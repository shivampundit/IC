📘 2.1 Debentures, DRR, DRF & Debenture Trustee Mandate (Section 71 & Rule 18)

Dividend out of Free Reserves in Year of Inadequate/Absence of Profits (Rule 3 of Declaration & Payment of Dividend Rules, 2014 — 4 Mandatory Conditions):
Condition 1 (Rate Cap): Declared Dividend Rate ≤ Average of dividend rates declared in 3 immediately preceding FYs (not applicable if no dividend was declared in each of the 3 preceding FYs)
Condition 2 (Withdrawal Cap): Total amount drawn from accumulated profits ≤ 1/10th (10%) of (Paid-up Share Capital + Free Reserves) as per latest audited financial statements

📘 3.1 Five Statutory Exceptions to Section 127 Penalty & Section 8 Company Prohibition

  • Five Exceptions where Non-Payment within 30 Days is NOT an Offence (Proviso to Sec 127):
    1. Where the dividend could not be paid by reason of the operation of any law;
    2. Where a shareholder has given directions to the company regarding the payment of the dividend and those directions cannot be complied with and the same has been communicated to him;
    3. Where there is a dispute regarding the right to receive the dividend;
    4. Where the dividend has been lawfully adjusted by the company against any sum due from the shareholder to the company (e.g., calls in arrears); or
    5. Where, for any other reason, the failure to pay the dividend or to post the warrant within the period under this section was not due to any default on the part of the company.
  • Section 8 Companies (Charitable/Non-Profit Companies): Strictly prohibited from paying any dividend to their members; all profits must be applied solely towards promoting their objects.
