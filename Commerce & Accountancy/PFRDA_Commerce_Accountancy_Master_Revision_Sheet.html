CH 02

Theory Base of Accounting — Concepts, Principles & Conventions

5 PYQs
🚀 2025–2026 Statutory, Regulatory & Current Affairs Updates
  • AS-1 Three Fundamental Accounting Assumptions (G-C-A Mnemonic): Under ICAI AS-1, there are ONLY THREE Fundamental Accounting Assumptions: (1) Going Concern, (2) Consistency, and (3) Accrual! If followed, no disclosure is needed; if ANY is not followed, non-compliance must be disclosed!
  • AS-1 Three Policy Selection Considerations: (1) Prudence (Conservatism), (2) Substance over Form, and (3) Materiality.
📋 Master Matrix of All Accounting Concepts & Conventions and Their Practical Applications
Accounting Concept / ConventionCore Principle & AssumptionDirect Accounting Application / EntryHigh-Yield Exam Trap / Exception
1. Business Entity ConceptBusiness enterprise is treated as a distinct accounting unit separate from its owners.Owner's capital is recorded as an internal LIABILITY; personal withdrawals are debited to Drawings A/c.Applies to ALL forms of business in accounting (even Sole Proprietorships!).
2. Money Measurement ConceptOnly transactions capable of being expressed in monetary terms are recorded.Excludes qualitative factors such as dedicated workforce, CEO health, brand loyalty, or managerial conflict.Assumes stable monetary unit (ignores inflation).
3. Going Concern Concept (AS-1 #1)Enterprise will continue in operation for the foreseeable future without liquidation.Justifies recording Fixed Assets at Historical Cost less Depreciation rather than liquidation value.If invalid, assets are valued at Net Realizable / Liquidation Value.
4. Periodicity & Matching ConceptsExpenses incurred in an accounting period must be matched against revenues earned in that period.Requires year-end adjustments for Outstanding/Prepaid Expenses, Accrued/Unearned Income, and Closing Stock.Revenue is recognized first (Realisation), and related costs are matched against it.
5. Conservatism (Prudence) Convention'Anticipate no profit, but provide for all possible losses.'Valuation of Inventories at Lower of Cost or NRV (AS-2); Provision for Doubtful Debts.Providing for Discount on Creditors violates Conservatism because it anticipates a future gain!

Materiality: Exception permitting insignificant items to be expensed.A ₹150 stapler lasting 5 years is expensed immediately to Stationery A/c instead of being depreciated.Materiality depends on both size and nature of the item.
📐 Key Formulas, Statutory Thresholds & Core Identities
Fundamental Accounting Equation (Dual Aspect Concept): Assets (A) = Equity / Capital (C) + Liabilities (L)
Expanded Accounting Equation: Closing Capital = Opening Capital + Additional Capital + Revenue − Expenses (Net Profit) − Drawings
🧮 Worked Numerical / Practical Case Study: Expanded Equation Numerical: Opening Assets = ₹8,00,000, Opening Liabilities = ₹3,00,000 (so Opening Capital = ₹5,00,000). Additional Capital = ₹1,00,000, Drawings = ₹60,000. Closing Assets = ₹10,50,000, Closing Liabilities = ₹3,50,000 (so Closing Capital = ₹7,00,000). Net Profit = ₹7,00,000 − (₹5,00,000 + ₹1,00,000 − ₹60,000) = ₹1,60,000!
CH 03

Recording of Transactions Part 1 — Vouchers, Golden Rules, Journal & Ledger

2 PYQs
🚀 2025–2026 Statutory, Regulatory & Current Affairs Updates
  • Representative Personal Account Rule: Whenever any prefix or suffix (Outstanding, Prepaid, Accrued, Unearned/Advance) is added to a Nominal Account (Salary, Rent, Interest, Insurance, Commission), it becomes a Representative Personal Account!
  • Trade Discount vs Cash Discount: Trade Discount is deducted on the invoice and NEVER entered in ledger accounts; Cash Discount is recorded as a Nominal Account.

CH 04

Recording of Transactions Part 2 — Cash Book, Subsidiary Books & Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS)

0 PYQs
🚀 2025–2026 Statutory, Regulatory & Current Affairs Updates
  • Cash Book Dual Role: Cash Book is BOTH a Book of Original Entry (Journal) AND a Principal Book (Ledger). Cash column can NEVER have a credit balance; Bank column CAN have a credit balance (Overdraft).
  • Subsidiary Book Exclusions: Purchases Book records ONLY credit purchases of goods (excludes cash purchases and credit purchases of fixed assets!).

📐 Key Formulas, Statutory Thresholds & Core Identities

🧮 Worked Numerical / Practical Case Study: Worked BRS with Overdraft: Overdraft as per Cash Book (Cr) = ₹45,000. Unpresented cheques = ₹12,000; Uncleared deposits = ₹18,000; Bank overdraft interest debited in Pass Book = ₹1,500; Direct deposit by customer = ₹6,500. Algebraic balance: `−45,000 + 12,000 − 18,000 − 1,500 + 6,500 = −₹46,000` → Overdraft (Debit Balance) as per Pass Book = ₹46,000!
CH 05

Trial Balance and Rectification of Errors (Suspense Account)

2 PYQs
🚀 2025–2026 Statutory, Regulatory & Current Affairs Updates
  • Trial Balance Agreement Rule: Two-sided errors (Complete Omission, Error of Principle, Compensating Errors, Wrong amount in Subsidiary Book, Posting to wrong account on the correct side) DO NOT affect Trial Balance agreement! One-sided errors (Partial omission, Wrong casting of subsidiary book, Posting to wrong side or wrong amount) DO affect Trial Balance agreement and require a Suspense Account.
  • Effect on Net Profit: Net Profit changes ONLY when a rectification entry debits or credits a Nominal Account!
📋 Classification of Accounting Errors & Impact on Trial Balance
Type of ErrorDefinition & MechanismDoes Trial Balance Agree?Concrete Exam Example
Error of PrincipleTreating Capital Expenditure as Revenue Expenditure (or vice versa).YES (Agrees).Installation wages of new machinery debited to Wages A/c instead of Machinery A/c.
Complete vs Partial OmissionComplete: Omitted from original book. Partial: Omitted from one ledger posting.Complete: YES. Partial: NO (Disagrees).Sale to Mohan recorded in Sales Book but not posted to Mohan's A/c.
Error of CommissionClerical error in casting, carry-forward, or posting.Wrong account on right side → YES; Wrong side/amount/casting → NO.Credit purchase of ₹7,500 from Ramesh debited as ₹5,700 to Ramesh → Disagrees by ₹13,200!
Compensating ErrorsTwo errors cancelling each other's Dr and Cr amounts.YES (Agrees).Purchases Book and Sales Book both undercast by ₹2,000.
📐 Key Formulas, Statutory Thresholds & Core Identities
Effect of Rectification on Net Profit: Nominal A/c Debited in Rectification → Net Profit Decreases | Nominal A/c Credited → Net Profit Increases | Only Real/Personal/Suspense A/cs → ZERO Effect on Net Profit
Next-Period Rectification: Replace every Nominal Account with 'Profit and Loss Adjustment Account'
🧮 Worked Numerical / Practical Case Study: Double Discrepancy Numerical: Credit purchase from Ramesh ₹7,500 was debited to Ramesh's A/c as ₹5,700. Trial Balance Debit exceeds Credit by `₹7,500 + ₹5,700 = ₹13,200`. Rectification: Dr Suspense A/c ₹13,200, Cr Ramesh's A/c ₹13,200 (Zero effect on Net Profit since neither is a Nominal Account!).
CH 06

Bills of Exchange and Promissory Notes

2 PYQs
🚀 2025–2026 Statutory, Regulatory & Current Affairs Updates
  • Public Holiday vs Emergency Holiday Maturity Rule (Sec 25 of NI Act, 1881): If maturity falls on a Scheduled / Public Holiday (Sunday, Aug 15, Oct 2, Jan 26) → due on the NEXT PRECEDING BUSINESS DAY (1 day earlier)! If it falls on an unforeseen Emergency Holiday → due on the NEXT SUCCEEDING BUSINESS DAY (1 day later)!
  • Noting Charges Rule: Always recoverable from Drawee (debited to Drawee's Personal A/c in Drawer's books, and debited as Noting Charges Expense in Drawee's books).
📋 Bill of Exchange (Sec 5) vs Promissory Note (Sec 4) & Dishonour Rules
DimensionBill of Exchange (Sec 5)Promissory Note (Sec 4)Universal Dishonour Rule in Drawer's Books
Creator, Nature & PartiesDrawn by Creditor (Drawer); unconditional ORDER to pay; 3 parties (Drawer, Drawee, Payee).Made by Debtor (Maker); unconditional PROMISE to pay; 2 parties (Maker, Payee).Cannot be made payable to bearer on demand (Sec 31 RBI Act).
Acceptance & LiabilityRequires formal acceptance by Drawee; Drawer's liability is secondary/conditional.No acceptance needed; Maker's liability is primary and absolute.In all 4 dishonour cases in Drawer's books: ALWAYS Debit Drawee's A/c (Bill + Noting Charges) and Credit Holder (B/R, Bank, Endorsee, or Bill for Collection)!
Retirement vs Renewal vs AccommodationRetirement: Early payment before maturity with Rebate (expense to Drawer, gain to Drawee).Renewal: Cancel old bill + charge interest for extended period + draw new bill.Accommodation Bills: Drawn for mutual financial help without trade consideration; discount shared in ratio of proceeds shared.
📐 Key Formulas, Statutory Thresholds & Core Identities
Maturity Date = Nominal Date + Tenure + 3 Days of Grace (Zero days of grace for bills payable On Demand / At Sight)
Bank Discount = Face Value × Discount Rate % × (Unexpired Period ÷ 12) | Rebate on Retirement = Face Value × Rebate Rate % × (Early Payment Period ÷ 12)
🧮 Worked Numerical / Practical Case Study: Maturity Date Numerical: Bill drawn on June 12, 2026 for 2 months after date. Nominal due date = Aug 12 + 3 days grace = Aug 15, 2026 (Independence Day — Public Holiday) → Legal due date is August 14, 2026 (preceding business day)!
CH 07

Financial Statements — Income Statement (Trading and Profit & Loss Account)

1 PYQs
🚀 2025–2026 Statutory, Regulatory & Current Affairs Updates
  • Direct Expenses (Trading A/c) vs Indirect Expenses (P&L A/c): Direct: Wages, Carriage/Freight Inward, Octroi, Customs Duty, Dock Charges, Factory Rent/Fuel/Power, Royalty on Production. Indirect: Salaries, Carriage Outward, Office Rent, Bad Debts, Depreciation, Packing for Sale, Royalty on Sales.
  • Closing Stock Inside Trial Balance Trap: If Closing Stock is given INSIDE the Trial Balance, it means it has already been deducted from Purchases (Adjusted Purchases), so it appears ONLY ONCE in the Balance Sheet (Current Asset) and NEVER in the Trading Account!
📋 Capital vs Revenue Expenditure & Receipts and Trading vs P&L Classification
CategoryCore CriterionHigh-Yield Exam ExamplesFinancial Statement Treatment
Capital vs Revenue ExpenditureCapital: Acquires fixed asset or enhances earning capacity/life (>1 yr). Revenue: Maintains existing capacity.Capital: Overhaul of 2nd-hand machine at purchase, installation wages, legal fee for land. Revenue: Routine repairs, whitewashing.Capital Exp → Balance Sheet Asset; Revenue Exp → Trading/P&L Debit.
Capital vs Revenue ReceiptsCapital: Creates liability (Loan/Shares) or reduces fixed asset (Sale of Land). Wages & Salaries vs Salaries & WagesFirst word dictates the account head.'Wages and Salaries' → Trading A/c (Direct); 'Salaries and Wages' → P&L A/c (Indirect).Freight/Carriage Inward → Trading A/c; Freight/Carriage Outward → P&L A/c.
📐 Key Formulas, Statutory Thresholds & Core Identities
Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) = Opening Stock + Net Purchases + Direct Expenses − Closing Stock
Gross Profit = Net Sales − COGS | Operating Profit (EBIT) = Gross Profit − Operating Expenses (Admin + Selling & Distribution)
🧮 Worked Numerical / Practical Case Study: COGS & Operating Profit Numerical: Op Stock ₹80k, Net Purchases ₹5,00k, Carriage Inward ₹15k, Wages ₹35k, Cl Stock ₹90k, Net Sales ₹7,50k, Carriage Outward ₹12k, Office Salaries ₹68k, Loss by Fire ₹10k. COGS = 80k + 500k + 50k − 90k = ₹5,40,000; Gross Profit = 750k − 540k = ₹2,10,000; Operating Profit = 210k − (12k + 68k) = ₹1,30,000!

5 PYQs
🚀 2025–2026 Statutory, Regulatory & Current Affairs Updates
  • Sequential Debtors Adjustment Chain: From Trial Balance Debtors: (1) First deduct Further Bad Debts (given outside TB) → (2) Compute New Provision for Doubtful Debts (%) on remaining Debtors → (3) Compute Provision for Discount on Debtors (%) on the remaining GOOD Debtors!
  • Manager's Commission Formula: Before charging = `NP × R/100`; After charging such commission = `NP × R/(100 + R)`.

🧮 Worked Numerical / Practical Case Study: Debtors & Commission Numerical: TB Debtors = ₹1,04,000; Further Bad Debts = ₹4,000 (Net = ₹1,00,000); New Prov for Doubtful Debts @ 5% = ₹5,000 (Good Debtors = ₹95,000); Prov for Discount @ 2% on ₹95,000 = ₹1,900 → Net Debtors in B/S = ₹93,100. If NP before commission is ₹1,10,000 and Manager gets 10% after charging commission: `1,10,000 × 10/110 = ₹10,000`.

Accounting for Share Capital Transactions (Issue, Forfeiture & Reissue)

5 PYQs
🚀 2025–2026 Statutory, Regulatory & Current Affairs Updates
  • Table F Interest Rates: Calls in Arrears = 10% p.a.; Calls in Advance = 12% p.a.

CH 12

Accounting for Bonus Issue and Rights Issue

11 PYQs
🚀 2025–2026 Statutory, Regulatory & Current Affairs Updates
  • Permitted vs Prohibited Reserves for Bonus Issue (Sec 63): Permitted: (1) Capital Redemption Reserve (CRR), (2) Securities Premium (in cash), (3) Capital Reserve (realized in cash), (4) Free Reserves / General Reserve / P&L. PROHIBITED: Revaluation Reserve and issue in lieu of dividend!
  • Impact of Bonus Issue on Net Worth & Debt-Equity Ratio: Bonus issue converts Reserves into Share Capital—so Total Shareholders' Equity (Net Worth) and Debt-Equity Ratio remain 100% UNCHANGED!
