Trial Balance and Rectification of Errors (Suspense Account)

Objectives of Trial Balance, Two-Sided vs One-Sided Errors, Classification of Errors (Omission, Commission, Principle, Compensating) & Effect of Rectification on Net Profit

🚀 2025–2026 Statutory, Regulatory & Current Affairs Updates
  • Trial Balance Agreement Rule: Two-sided errors (Complete Omission, Error of Principle, Compensating Errors, Wrong amount in Subsidiary Book, Posting to wrong account on the correct side) DO NOT affect Trial Balance agreement! One-sided errors (Partial omission, Wrong casting of subsidiary book, Posting to wrong side or wrong amount) DO affect Trial Balance agreement and require a Suspense Account.
  • Effect on Net Profit: Net Profit changes ONLY when a rectification entry debits or credits a Nominal Account!

🚀 2025–2026 Statutory, Regulatory & Current Affairs Updates
  • Cross-Transaction Rule (7 Official PYQs!): A flow of funds occurs ONLY when one account is a Current Account (CA or CL) and the other is a Non-Current Account (Fixed Asset, Long-Term Debt, or Equity)!
